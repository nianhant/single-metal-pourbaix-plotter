Required table
Complete measured_conditions_to_complete.csv for all seven electrodes.
For each endpoint obtain bulk pH, the paired achieved iR-corrected potential,
sampling time, uncertainties if available, and the original record reference.
Record the actual time represented by "initial": an unpolarized feed pH has
no paired applied electrode potential. If using feed pH with the first
polarized potential, state that pairing explicitly rather than implying a
simultaneous measurement. Add intermediate measured points if available.

If only raw Hg/HgO potentials are available, obtain the simultaneous signed
current and uncompensated resistance and establish how much compensation
the instrument already applied. Under the usual anodic-positive convention,
E_interface,SHE = E_recorded,Hg/HgO + E_reference,SHE - I*Ru;
E_interface,RHE = E_interface,SHE + (2.303 RT/F)*pH.
Verify the instrument convention and avoid correcting twice. The SI wording
"iR drop was added back" needs clarification; a compliance voltage is a
whole-cell/instrument limit, not Cu's working-electrode potential vs RHE.

How to draw the requested conditions
Fig 1: Au, Cu, Ni, Pd receive their corresponding measured points. Pt and
Ti are computational comparisons here; do not invent experimental points.
Fig 3: TiNi, TiCu, PdAu receive their respective measured points.
Use an open circle for the first polarized condition and a filled circle
for 120 min, with a thin connecting arrow denoting endpoint order only.
If intermediate paired records exist, plot them and label selected times.
Do not claim that a straight endpoint connector is a measured trajectory.
Use a black or contrasting marker with a white halo to preserve visibility
on phase colors. Add x/y error bars only where uncertainties are supported.
On existing SHE axes, plot each point at
    (bulk_pH, E_RHE_iR_corrected - 0.05916*bulk_pH), at 298.15 K.
Use one consistent temperature-dependent prefactor in production code.
Remove the old broad "experimental window", or label it as a separate
screening envelope. A common nominal potential band cannot replace points.
The earlier generated SVG bands are layout studies and must not be submitted
as measured-condition overlays, particularly for Cu.
