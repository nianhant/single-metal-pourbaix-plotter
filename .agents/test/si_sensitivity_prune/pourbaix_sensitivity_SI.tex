The concentration sensitivity of Au, Ni, Cu, and Pd was examined at 298.15~K by varying one concentration at a time. The nominal aqueous metal-ion concentration was varied from $10^{-6}$ to $10^{-2}\,\mathrm{M}$, the total ammonia concentration from $0.002$ to $0.1\,\mathrm{M}$, and the total glycine concentration from $0.01$ to $1.0\,\mathrm{M}$. Other concentrations were held at their reference values: nominal aqueous metal-ion concentration $10^{-4}\,\mathrm{M}$, $[\ce{NH3}]_{\mathrm{tot}}=0.02\,\mathrm{M}$, and $[\mathrm{Gly}]_{\mathrm{tot}}=0.1\,\mathrm{M}$, without added cyanide. The nominal metal-ion concentration corresponds to the activity input under the ideal-solution convention. Each black outline marks pH 10.5--13.5 and $-2.0$ to $2.3$~V vs RHE, converted to SHE and clipped to the plotted range. Sweeps without a discernible change in the phase map are omitted.
